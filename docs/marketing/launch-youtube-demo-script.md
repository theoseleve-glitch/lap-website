# Launch YouTube demo script

A 2-minute YouTube demo script for the LAP public-beta launch on 2026-07-23. Scene-by-scene shot list with **Visual:** and **Narration:** fields per scene; total runtime ≈2:00. Drafted today (2026-04-26, Month 1) and recorded once Driver OS v1 is captureable in late June 2026, posted on launch day.

Voice is founder-direct, not voice-of-god marketing. **Visual** cues describe what the editor needs to show on screen; **Narration** is what the founder reads to the camera. Date-anchored copy ("LAP enters public beta on 2026-07-23") reads accurately whether the cut posts ahead of launch (preview) or after (catalogue piece) — at launch the founder lightly tweaks one verb tense, no other content changes.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:15)

**Visual:** Quick split-screen montage. Left half: phone clipped to a real-track dashboard, lap timer running, helmet visible at frame edge. Right half: an iRacing GT3 cockpit on a 32:9 monitor at the same circuit. Both halves cross-fade to a single "LAP" wordmark on a black ground.

**Narration:** "If you race a sim on Tuesday and a real circuit on Sunday, your driver record probably lives in two completely separate places that never talk to each other. LAP fixes that — one per-driver record across both."

## Scene 2 — Capture across platforms (0:15–0:45)

**Visual:** Phone screen capture. User opens LAP app, taps "New session," picks "iRacing — Suzuka GP." Quick cut to phone clipped to dashboard at a real circuit; "Track-day — Vallelunga" tile appears in the same session list. Cross-fade to a single per-driver dashboard with both sessions stacked, time-stamped a day apart.

**Narration:** "LAP captures sessions from iRacing, ACC, AMS2, and rFactor 2. Add real-track captures from a phone GPS or a RaceBox and the same per-driver record extends across sim and real-world racing. Cross-platform from day one — that's the framing the whole product is built on."

## Scene 3 — Diagnosis from a written rule (0:45–1:15)

**Visual:** Per-session breakdown opens. Cursor hovers over the corner labelled "Turn 4 — La Source." Data overlay shows the brake-release shape across all attempts against a corner-segment baseline. A diagnosis card appears: "Released the brake too early at La Source across 5 of 7 attempts." Tap the card; the underlying rule expands with predicate / trigger / prescription / success-criterion fields filled in.

**Narration:** "The coaching itself is hand-curated by humans, not generated by a language model. Every diagnosis traces to a written rule the team maintains in public — predicate, trigger, prescription, success criterion, all four. So when LAP tells you you released too early at La Source, you can read exactly which rule fired and why."

## Scene 4 — Drill with a measurable success criterion (1:15–1:40)

**Visual:** Diagnosis card flips to a "Next session" prescription card. Drill name reads: "Brake-release calibration — Turn 4." Success criterion: "Hold full brake pressure 0.3s longer through apex on 4 of next 5 attempts." User taps "Add to plan"; the development plan stack updates to show the drill alongside two prior open drills.

**Narration:** "Each diagnosis comes with a named drill and a measurable success criterion to attempt next session. No leaderboards, no setup widgets, no gamification surfaces. Just a per-driver development plan you can actually execute on track between now and your next session."

## Scene 5 — Launch and waitlist CTA (1:40–2:00)

**Visual:** Wordmark on black returns. Three lines fade in stacked: "Public beta · 2026-07-23". "€19.90/month, 30-day trial". "Founding 50: €14.90/month." Bottom card: "lap-coach.racing/waitlist".

**Narration:** "LAP enters public beta on 2026-07-23. Nineteen ninety per month after launch with a 30-day free trial. Join the waitlist for a head start to claim one of fifty founding spots at fourteen ninety a month. Link in the description below."
