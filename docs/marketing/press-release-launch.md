# LAP launches public beta of its cross-platform driver-development surface for sim and real-world racing

_European sim racers and track-day drivers get a single coaching surface across iRacing, Assetto Corsa Competizione, AMS2, rFactor 2, and on-track sessions — with no language-model coaching narrative._

[CITY] — July 23, 2026 — LAP today opened the public beta of its driver-development platform, giving sim racers and real-world track-day drivers in Europe one place where every session — captured on iRacing, Assetto Corsa Competizione, AMS2, or with a GPS device on a real circuit — becomes a per-driver development plan with a named drill, a measurable success criterion, and a review cadence.

Driver development today is fragmented. Sim telemetry tools sit on one side, lap timing and on-track apps on the other, and the conclusions a driver reaches in the simulator rarely transfer cleanly to the real circuit. LAP closes that gap by treating both as captures of the same driver. The brake-zone ordering at Spa La Source, the trail-brake release shape at the Suzuka hairpin, and the consistency variance across a five-corner sequence are diagnosed identically whether the session was a Tuesday-evening league race on iRacing or a Sunday track day at Brands Hatch. One per-driver record follows the driver across platforms.

LAP's coaching is not produced by a language model. Every prescription a driver receives traces to a hand-curated written rule that the LAP coaching team maintains in public — version-controlled, defensible to a parent or a programme director, and auditable when a driver asks why a particular drill was recommended. The discipline is a deliberate scope choice. A written rule library is the only coaching artefact that can be trusted at the depth a driver needs to act on, and LAP commits in writing to that posture for the foreseeable horizon of the product.

LAP launches with a single consumer tier at €19.90 per month, monthly only, and a 30-day free trial. The first 50 founding members lock €14.90 per month — €5 under standard — for as long as the membership stays active. Multi-seat pricing for racing schools and karting academies starts from €10 per seat per month for programmes of ten or more drivers, finalised in conversation with each operator. There is no advertising-supported free tier and no plan to introduce one.

Today's public beta is followed by the V1 release on September 23, 2026, which adds the Android application, an Apple Watch and WearOS companion, version one of the coaching library with more than one hundred hand-curated rules, and explicit endurance-event support. The active development log and the public roadmap are published openly at lap-coach.racing/blog so journalists, drivers, and operators can follow the work in detail rather than waiting for the next release announcement.

## About LAP

LAP is the cross-platform driver-development surface for sim racers and real-world track-day drivers. The product captures every sim and real-world session, identifies the technique weakness shape behind each, and emits a per-driver development plan with named drills and measurable success criteria. LAP runs across desktop, mobile, web, and watch surfaces. The company is in pre-launch development from Month 1 of 2026 and ships the public beta on July 23, 2026, followed by V1 on September 23, 2026. lap-coach.racing
