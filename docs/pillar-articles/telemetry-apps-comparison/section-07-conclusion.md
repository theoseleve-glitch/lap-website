## Conclusion

There is no single best telemetry app. The right answer
depends on your persona, your budget, and your platform mix
— and on whether your driving lives on a sim, on a real
circuit, or across both.

The article walked six apps (LAP, Garage61, RaceLab, MoTeC,
Track Attack, STINT) through six shared dimensions: sim
capture surface, real-world capture surface, data depth,
cross-platform unification, pricing model, and persona fit.
Section 5 mapped persona to shortlist; Section 6 listed our
own current limits.
